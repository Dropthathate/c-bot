About SomaSync AI

Documentation built for the work that cannot pause.

SomaSync AI is a hands-free clinical documentation workflow designed specifically for manual therapists and neuromuscular therapists. It helps practitioners keep session context moving while keeping review, clinical judgment, and final documentation in the clinician’s hands.

Our operating principle

Technology should support the treatment room, not interrupt it.

Manual therapy depends on attention, touch, observation, and continuity. SomaSync AI is being built around those realities—not around generic dictation workflows.

Every draft remains clinician-reviewable. SomaSync AI does not make clinical decisions or submit documentation automatically.

Designed around the practitioner.

The goal is not to turn a session into a transcription task. The goal is to support accurate, timely documentation without breaking the flow of hands-on care.

01 / HANDS-FREE

Keep treatment moving.

A compatible hands-free workflow is intended to reduce the need to step away, type, or reconstruct session context later.

02 / PURPOSE-BUILT

Built for manual therapy.

SomaSync AI is focused on the practical realities of bodywork and neuromuscular therapy, rather than being a generic voice-note product.

03 / CLINICIAN-LED

Review remains essential.

Generated documentation is a working draft. The clinician reviews, edits, and approves the final record according to their own standards and requirements.

A clearer documentation flow.

SomaSync AI is being developed as a connected workflow—from real-time session context to a structured, clinician-reviewed note.

01

Connect

Use a compatible hands-free setup designed to fit naturally into the treatment environment.

02

Capture context

Maintain session context while care continues, without relying on memory at the end of the day.

03

Structure the draft

Organize the documentation draft around the clinician’s preferred SOAP-note workflow.

04

Review and finalize

The clinician reviews and edits the result before any documentation is used or stored.

Care is personal. The documentation process should respect that.
