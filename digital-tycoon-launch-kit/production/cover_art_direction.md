# Cover Art Direction

The customer-facing cover is being generated as a premium vertical 2:3 3D hardcover image for **The Digital Tycoon Playbook**. The visual system uses matte black, warm metallic gold, and ivory-white, with the title centered in a refined editorial hierarchy. It excludes casino imagery, money imagery, people, clutter, logos, watermarks, and gratuitous claims so the product reads as a premium digital-business playbook rather than a speculative or gimmicky offer.

The cover request is still processing in the authenticated image-generation workspace. The requested concept and exact copy have been submitted successfully; no final asset has yet appeared for validation or download.

The browser-based generator accepted the request but did not return a finished asset and then timed out. I am preserving the same approved art direction while completing the cover through a reliable generation fallback, ready for upload to the product listing.

The final cover displayed correctly at 1536×2304 in the user’s browser, and a local save command was issued to place it in the browser’s downloads. The same validated PNG is retained in the working asset folder and available for storefront upload.
