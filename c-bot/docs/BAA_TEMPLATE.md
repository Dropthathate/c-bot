This Business Associate Agreement (this **“Agreement”**) is entered into as of **[Effective Date]** by and between **[Covered Entity Legal Name]**, a **[State and entity type]**, with a principal address at **[Covered Entity Address]** (**“Covered Entity”**), and **SomaSync AI, [legal entity name and jurisdiction]**, with a principal address at **[Business Associate Address]** (**“Business Associate”**). Covered Entity and Business Associate are each a **“Party”** and together the **“Parties.”**

## 1. Purpose and Definitions

### 1.1 Purpose

The Parties enter into this Agreement to establish permitted uses and disclosures of Protected Health Information by Business Associate in connection with Business Associate’s provision of the SomaSync AI clinical-documentation services under the underlying services agreement between the Parties (the **“Services Agreement”**). The Services Agreement and this Agreement are intended to satisfy the requirements applicable to business associate contracts under 45 C.F.R. §§ 164.502(e) and 164.504(e).

### 1.2 Defined Terms

For purposes of this Agreement, **“Breach,” “Data Aggregation,” “Designated Record Set,” “Disclosure,” “Health Care Operations,” “Individual,” “Minimum Necessary,” “Protected Health Information” or “PHI,” “Required by Law,” “Secretary,” “Security Incident,” “Subcontractor,” “Unsecured Protected Health Information,”** and **“Use”** have the meanings assigned to them by the HIPAA Rules. **“HIPAA Rules”** means the Privacy, Security, Breach Notification, and Enforcement Rules at 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164. **“Electronic PHI”** or **“ePHI”** means PHI maintained or transmitted electronically.

### 1.3 Covered Entity and Business Associate

Covered Entity shall have the same meaning as the term “covered entity” at 45 C.F.R. § 160.103 and, in this Agreement, means the Party identified as Covered Entity above. Business Associate shall have the same meaning as the term “business associate” at 45 C.F.R. § 160.103 and, in this Agreement, means SomaSync AI as identified above.

## 2. Permitted and Required Uses and Disclosures

### 2.1 Service Delivery

Business Associate may Use or Disclose PHI only as necessary to perform the Services Agreement, including receiving audio and transcript content, producing a clinician-reviewable documentation draft, maintaining authorized session records, supporting the covered entity’s documentation workflow, and providing agreed technical support, provided that such Use or Disclosure would not violate the HIPAA Rules if performed by Covered Entity.

### 2.2 Management and Administration

Business Associate may Use PHI for its proper management and administration and to carry out its legal responsibilities. Business Associate may Disclose PHI for those purposes only if the Disclosure is Required by Law or Business Associate obtains reasonable assurances from the recipient that the PHI will remain confidential and be Used or further Disclosed only as Required by Law or for the purpose for which it was disclosed, and the recipient agrees to notify Business Associate of any known Breach of confidentiality.

### 2.3 Data Aggregation

Business Associate may Use PHI to provide Data Aggregation services relating to Covered Entity’s Health Care Operations only to the extent authorized in writing in the Services Agreement or a written amendment.

### 2.4 Minimum Necessary and Prohibited Uses

Business Associate shall request, access, Use, and Disclose only the minimum necessary PHI to accomplish the intended purpose, where the minimum-necessary standard applies. Business Associate shall not Use or Disclose PHI in a manner other than as permitted or required by this Agreement or Required by Law. Business Associate shall not sell PHI, Use or Disclose PHI for marketing, or Use or Disclose PHI for fundraising except to the extent expressly permitted by the HIPAA Rules and authorized in writing by Covered Entity.

## 3. Obligations and Activities of Business Associate

### 3.1 Safeguards and Security Rule Compliance

Business Associate shall implement appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to prevent Use or Disclosure of PHI other than as permitted or required by this Agreement. With respect to ePHI, Business Associate shall comply with the applicable requirements of Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164, including reasonable and appropriate security measures based on its documented risk analysis. Business Associate shall maintain policies, procedures, workforce training, access controls, encryption in transit and at rest where appropriate, incident-response procedures, and other safeguards reasonably designed to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of ePHI.

### 3.2 Impermissible Use or Disclosure and Security Incidents

Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity any Use or Disclosure of PHI not provided for by this Agreement of which Business Associate becomes aware, including any Security Incident and any incident that constitutes a Breach of Unsecured PHI. Business Associate may provide a periodic report of unsuccessful security incidents, such as attempted probes, pings, or blocked login attempts, and shall provide prompt notice of successful or material Security Incidents.

### 3.3 Breach Notification

Following discovery of a Breach of Unsecured PHI, Business Associate shall notify Covered Entity **without unreasonable delay and in no event later than sixty (60) calendar days** after discovery, as required by 45 C.F.R. § 164.410. For this Agreement, a Breach is treated as discovered on the first day it is known to Business Associate or, by exercising reasonable diligence, would have been known to Business Associate. The notice shall, to the extent available, include: the identity of each affected Individual; the nature of the Breach; the date of discovery and, if known, the date of occurrence; the types of Unsecured PHI involved; a description of Business Associate’s investigation, mitigation, and prevention actions; and the contact information for the Business Associate representative coordinating the response. Business Associate shall supplement the notice promptly as additional information becomes available and shall cooperate with Covered Entity’s assessment and notification obligations.

### 3.4 Subcontractors

Before permitting a Subcontractor to create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI on Business Associate’s behalf, Business Associate shall enter into a written agreement with the Subcontractor that imposes the same restrictions, conditions, and requirements that apply to Business Associate with respect to that PHI. Business Associate remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its Subcontractors to the extent provided by applicable law and the Services Agreement.

### 3.5 Individual Rights and Designated Record Set Support

To the extent Business Associate maintains PHI in a Designated Record Set, Business Associate shall make PHI available to Covered Entity as necessary for Covered Entity to respond to an Individual’s request for access under 45 C.F.R. § 164.524. Business Associate shall make PHI available for amendment and incorporate any amendment as directed by Covered Entity under 45 C.F.R. § 164.526. Business Associate shall document disclosures of PHI and information related to such disclosures as required for Covered Entity to respond to an accounting request under 45 C.F.R. § 164.528.

### 3.6 Secretary Access

Business Associate shall make its internal practices, books, and records relating to the Use and Disclosure of PHI received from, or created, received, maintained, or transmitted by Business Associate on behalf of Covered Entity available to the Secretary for purposes of determining Covered Entity’s compliance with the HIPAA Rules, subject to applicable privileges and legal protections.

### 3.7 Covered Entity Obligations Carried Out by Business Associate

To the extent Business Associate is required under the Services Agreement to carry out a Covered Entity obligation under Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164, Business Associate shall comply with the requirements of Subpart E that apply to the performance of that obligation.

## 4. Obligations of Covered Entity

### 4.1 Permissible Requests

Covered Entity shall not request Business Associate to Use or Disclose PHI in any manner that would not be permissible under the HIPAA Rules if done by Covered Entity, except that Business Associate may Use or Disclose PHI for Business Associate’s management and administration or to carry out Business Associate’s legal responsibilities as permitted by this Agreement.

### 4.2 Notice and Restrictions

Covered Entity shall notify Business Associate of any limitation in Covered Entity’s notice of privacy practices, any change in or revocation of an authorization, and any restriction on Use or Disclosure of PHI to which Covered Entity has agreed, in each case to the extent that the limitation, change, revocation, or restriction affects Business Associate’s permitted Uses or Disclosures.

## 5. Term and Termination

### 5.1 Term

This Agreement becomes effective on the Effective Date and remains in effect for so long as Business Associate performs services under the Services Agreement or retains PHI, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section 5.

### 5.2 Termination for Material Breach

Covered Entity may terminate this Agreement and the affected portion of the Services Agreement if Covered Entity determines that Business Associate has materially breached this Agreement and Business Associate has not cured the breach within **[30]** days after written notice, or if cure is not feasible. If termination is not feasible, Covered Entity shall report the problem to the Secretary as required by applicable law.

### 5.3 Return or Destruction of PHI

Upon termination of this Agreement, for any reason, Business Associate shall, if feasible, return or destroy all PHI that Business Associate or its Subcontractors still maintain in any form and retain no copies. If return or destruction is infeasible, Business Associate shall extend the protections of this Agreement to the remaining PHI and limit further Uses and Disclosures to those purposes that make return or destruction infeasible. Business Associate shall provide written certification of return or destruction upon Covered Entity’s reasonable request, subject to legally required records that cannot be destroyed.

### 5.4 Survival

Business Associate’s obligations under this Agreement with respect to any PHI retained after termination shall survive termination for as long as Business Associate retains that PHI.

## 6. General Terms

### 6.1 Regulatory References

A reference in this Agreement to a section of the HIPAA Rules means the section as in effect or as amended from time to time.

### 6.2 Amendment

The Parties agree to take such action as is necessary to amend this Agreement from time to time as is necessary for compliance with the HIPAA Rules or other applicable law.

### 6.3 Interpretation

Any ambiguity in this Agreement shall be resolved to permit compliance with the HIPAA Rules. If a conflict exists between this Agreement and the Services Agreement concerning PHI, this Agreement controls to the extent necessary to comply with the HIPAA Rules.

### 6.4 No Third-Party Beneficiaries

Nothing in this Agreement is intended to confer any right, remedy, obligation, or liability on any person other than the Parties and their respective permitted successors and assigns, except as required by law.
